Designing for Process, Not Just Polish

Research board — five secondary-research streams + a 51-respondent primary survey, laid out as an empathy map, an affinity map, and a research synthesis.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

How might we design a social platform for creative people that values the depth of their process and skill — not just the polish of their final visuals — while giving them a dedicated space to discover and browse creative work within their own interests, free from unrelated noise?

THE MARKET GAP

No existing platform combines process-first content, non-engagement-optimized recognition, and niche/interest-based discovery in one product. Are.na owns the first; Behance/Dribbble/ArtStation own the third; nobody owns both.

Feature Pillars

Process-First Content

  • "Build Log" post type: sketch → draft → final
  • Own ranking surface, never merged with polish feed
  • Stage-pinned critique threads

Noise-Free Discovery

  • Opt-in tags/channels by medium or niche
  • No algorithmic virality feed, no ads
  • Transparent discovery logic

Recognition Beyond Likes

  • Skill-tagged reactions naming what worked
  • Depth-of-engagement ranking over raw views
  • Visible growth trail per creator

Trust & Craft Integrity

  • Optional human/AI provenance tagging
  • Peer recognition weighted by discipline
  • No pay-to-boost mechanics
